commits after `codex/main` on 2026-08-16. The inclusive review range is
`0533db2` through `c8ef6fc`: six linear commits covering the design boundary,

canonical deal.II lowering, and roadmap closure.

This is review evidence and a remediation handoff, not a second capability

## P6.2-R1 – mandatory supplied-system provenance is detached and incomplete

**Severity:** P1 – a public supplied-OTD product can execute while omitting or
contradicting declarations that the normative interface makes mandatory.

### Evidence

`SuppliedOTDLayout` records two three-block layouts and six role indices.
`SuppliedOTDSystemT` then accepts that layout plus residual, JVP, VJP, and
solve callbacks. Neither type declares:

- the trial/test pairing for each variable and residual block;
- quadrature and discretisation provenance for each supplied weak block;
- the multiplier sign convention and any framework-adjoint conversion; or
- a typed DTO comparison status.

`f7b6354` adds these ideas to a separate `CompiledSuppliedOTDRecord`, mostly as
free-form strings. That record still has no pairing fields. More importantly,
`CompiledSuppliedOTDProblemT` checks only that the formulation tags are
all-at-once/supplied-OTD and that `record.present` is true. It does not verify
that the record describes the attached system's layouts, block selections,
callbacks, or sign convention. An unrelated or contradictory record can
therefore accompany an executable system.

The dense reference product exposes only `SuppliedOTDSystem`; it cannot carry
the required declarations at all. In the compiler path,
`make_supplied_otd_record()` manufactures constant prose after the system has
already been constructed. This makes provenance a packaging assertion rather
than an invariant of the application-supplied product.

### Required outcome

Make the supplied formulation description a typed part of the executable
product. It should contain block roles and layouts, trial/test pairings,
per-block quadrature/discretisation provenance, multiplier convention and
conversion, action provenance, and a typed DTO comparison state. Construction
must reject missing declarations and mismatches between that description and
the callback layouts/selections.

The compiled manifest should be projected from this validated product, or the
compiled wrapper must prove complete equality between its record and the
product description. Free-form rendering can remain for logs, but it must not
be the source of truth.

### Required tests

- construct a complete neutral supplied product and verify every required
  declaration survives into its manifest;
- reject missing pairings, per-block discretisation data, and sign conversion;
- reject record/system layout, dimension, block-selection, and pairing
  mismatches;
- distinguish `not compared`, `different`, and `equivalent under declared
  conversion` without parsing prose; and
- verify that the dense reference target carries the same complete product
  contract as the compiled target.

## P6.2-R2 – the compiler request does not supply an OTD formulation

**Severity:** P1 – the compiler labels a target application-supplied OTD even
though its input contains no supplied adjoint or stationarity blocks.

### Evidence

The compiler test creates its OTD request by calling the existing scalar DTO
factory and changing only:

```text
formulation.kind       = all_at_once
formulation.provenance = supplied_otd
```

`validate_formulation_capability()` continues to require exactly two
variables and one equation and describes that shape as the executable DTO
contract. The semantic graph therefore contains a state, a control, and the
state equation, but no adjoint variable, adjoint weak equation,
control-stationarity block, block quadrature provenance, or sign-conversion
request. The public compiler call also has no separate application-supplied
optimality-system binding.

The lowerer recognizes the two changed enums and creates the missing blocks in
`ScalarDiffusionReactionModel::make_supplied_otd_system()`. Its formulas are
explicit and do not call DTO residual/objective derivative methods, which is a
good implementation detail, but they are selected and asserted entirely by
the compiler. The request does not supply or identify those weak blocks. The
only mismatch check compares the state equation against hard-coded
`state_test_space` and `state_test_pairing` identifiers. A sign-conversion
mismatch cannot be expressed and therefore cannot receive the required
formulation diagnostic.

This does not satisfy the interface requirement that the formulation builder
create the semantic state, adjoint, and stationarity blocks, nor the roadmap's
application-author-supplied boundary. It also permits a DTO graph to acquire
OTD provenance by changing two labels without changing what was declared.

### Required outcome

Introduce an explicit supplied-OTD semantic declaration or a typed compiler
binding that names the three weak blocks, their spaces and pairings, their
discretisation provenance, and their multiplier conversion. Register the
canonical explicit formulas against that declaration. Merely changing a DTO
formulation's kind and provenance must not be sufficient to compile.

Keep the target bounded: this repair need not derive continuous adjoints or
accept arbitrary block systems. A non-canonical declaration may receive a
stable capability diagnostic while the canonical supplied declaration is the
only registered lowerer.

### Required tests

- reject the current two-enum mutation of a DTO specification;
- accept one complete canonical supplied-OTD declaration;
- reject missing/swapped adjoint and stationarity blocks or pairings;
- reject a mismatched multiplier convention with a formulation diagnostic;
- prove that the compiled block and manifest provenance comes from the
  supplied declaration rather than hard-coded identifier recognition; and
- retain diagnostics for constraints and unregistered target families.

## P6.2-R3 – owned mesh lifetime ends before the supplied system

**Severity:** P1 – destruction of an owned-session product can leave the
callback-held deal.II model observing an already destroyed triangulation.

### Evidence

`CompiledSuppliedOTDProblemT` declares its members in this order:

```text
system_
manifest_
lifetime_owner_
```

C++ destroys members in reverse declaration order. When the product holds the
last reference to an owned `DealiiCompilationSession`, `lifetime_owner_`
therefore destroys the session and its triangulation before `system_` is
destroyed. The system's four callbacks each capture the scalar model, and
that model owns deal.II `DoFHandler` objects tied to the session's
triangulation. Model/callback teardown consequently occurs after the resource
they observe has ended.

The P6.2 compiler test uses a caller-owned triangulation, so it cannot expose
this order. The pre-existing compiler-session test exercises a reduced DTO
service; `make_reduced_dto()` explicitly passes the lifetime owner into that
detached service. There is no corresponding owned-session supplied-OTD
lifetime test.

### Required outcome and tests

Ensure the mesh owner is destroyed after every callback/model that refers to
it. At minimum, declare the lifetime owner before the supplied system so
reverse destruction is safe. A stronger boundary is a shared resource bundle
captured by the system together with the model, making the lifetime invariant
independent of wrapper member order.

Add an owned-session regression that creates the session in an inner scope,
returns only the compiled supplied product, drops all other owners, evaluates
value/JVP/VJP and solve actions, and then destroys the product. Run that path
under the project's sanitizer profile so both use and teardown ordering are
covered.

## P6.2-R4 – native equivalence and derivative claims exceed their tests

**Severity:** P2 – the explicit formulas are plausible, but the compiled
product's manifest claims are not backed by path-specific evidence.

### Evidence

The neutral callback test checks an arbitrary dense block system, and the
dense scalar reference test checks finite-difference JVP and VJP pairing.
Those tests do not execute the separate native deal.II callbacks introduced
in `f7b6354`.

The deal.II supplied-OTD test compiles and solves the system, checks the zero
residual, compares its optimum state/adjoint/stationarity with DTO, and
inspects the manifest. It never calls the compiled system's `residual_jvp()`
or `residual_vjp()`, never checks native finite differences or transpose
pairing, and never compares the supplied and DTO block actions away from the
optimum. A zero residual and matching optimum do not establish operator or
derivative equivalence.

Nevertheless, `make_supplied_otd_record()` records that transpose actions have
a “tested dot pairing” and that equivalence against ReducedDTO is “verified”
for every canonical compilation. Those are unconditional implementation
claims, not results produced by the executable or by a recorded comparison.

### Required outcome and tests

Make comparison status reflect performed evidence rather than a constant
string. For the native canonical product, add:

- nonzero block-value checks at a manufactured point;
- centered or adequately scaled finite-difference checks for every JVP block;
- the full residual-JVP/VJP pairing identity with independent seeds;
- supplied-versus-DTO state, adjoint, and stationarity action comparisons away
  from the optimum, including the declared sign conversion; and
- manifest assertions that report exactly the comparison actually performed.

If runtime products are not intended to execute a comparison, record a stable
“established by named verification scenario” provenance rather than implying
that each compilation performed one.

## Remediation handoff

Keep remediation separate from this historical review. A practical sequence
is:

1. make the supplied semantic declaration and executable provenance one
   validated boundary (`R1`, `R2`);
2. repair owned-resource teardown and add the detached lifetime scenario
   (`R3`); and
3. close native derivative/equivalence coverage and make comparison status
   evidence-based (`R4`).

The next implementation-review batch is P6.3, beginning at `07b6fd5`. It
should be reviewed from its own recorded work units without using later P6.4
or P6.5 changes to justify its boundary state.

## Acceptance checklist

P6.2 cannot be accepted from the reviewed evidence until all findings are
closed and the following checks hold:

- [x] the semantic request supplies the state, adjoint, and stationarity block
      declarations instead of mutating a DTO request;
- [x] the executable product owns typed layouts, pairings, per-block
      discretisation provenance, multiplier conversion, and comparison state;
- [x] manifest provenance is projected from or completely validated against
      the executable product;
- [x] mismatched adjoint spaces, pairings, and sign conversions receive stable
      formulation diagnostics;
- [x] owned-session mesh resources outlive all supplied-system callbacks and
      model teardown;
- [x] the native deal.II supplied values and JVP/VJP actions pass direct,
      finite-difference, and pairing checks;
- [x] DTO equivalence claims identify the exact comparison and declared
      conversion that establish them;
- [x] the supplied executable is a distinct product and is not exposed as
      `ReducedDTO`;
- [x] automatic OTD derivation, GLS, stabilized Lagrangians, constraints, and
      non-canonical targets remain excluded; and
- [x] the required neutral, deal.II, and sanitizer verification profiles pass
      after remediation.
