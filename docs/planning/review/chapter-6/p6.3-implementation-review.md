# P6.3 implementation review and remediation handoff

## Status and authority

This document records the completed static review of the P6.3 implementation
commits after `codex/main` on 2026-08-16. The inclusive review range is
`07b6fd5` through `b2511f1`: six linear commits matching the six work units
recorded in the implementation roadmap.

This is review evidence and a remediation handoff, not a second capability
ledger. The [implementation roadmap](../../implementation-roadmap.md) remains
the sole owner of current feature status and acceptance state. The
[Chapter 6 numerical-methods guide](../../../guides/chapter-6-numerical-methods.md),
[interface specification](../../../design/interface-specification.md), and
[v1 semantic compiler](../../../implementation/v1/semantic-compiler.md)
remain authoritative for KKT mathematics, executable composition, and
compiler registration.

The history establishes most of the selected algebraic slice: a generic
equality-constrained quadratic product, explicit $Q$, $D$, $D^{\mathsf T}$,
KKT and transpose actions, multiplier conversion, dense DTO and supplied-OTD
adapters, a named serial deal.II block realization, and identity-preconditioned
MINRES/GMRES solves with separate residual fields. P6.3 is not
acceptance-complete from the reviewed state. Four P1 compiler, pairing,
adapter, or ownership defects and one P2 verification/policy gap remain.

  equality action rather than a named PDE class;

## P6.3-R1 – the selected KKT target is not a compiler product

**Severity:** P1 – the roadmap closes a registered compilation slice that the
semantic compiler cannot request, validate, manifest, or return.

### Evidence

No P6.3 commit changes the semantic graph, `DealiiCompiler`,
`CompiledProblemT`, `CompiledSuppliedOTDProblemT`, `CompilationResultT`, or
`CompilationManifest`. At `b2511f1`, the compilation result can return only a
reduced DTO product or the P6.2 supplied-OTD product. There is no compiled KKT
product and no KKT manifest record.

The deal.II tests bypass that boundary. They construct
`ScalarDiffusionReactionModel` directly, pass it to
`ScalarDiffusionReactionKKT`, and invoke the KKT solver on the resulting
product. The semantic scalar specification, resolved request, compilation
session, and compiler diagnostics do not participate.

Consequently:

- the compiler does not construct the KKT action from the selected
  objective/residual services;
- the semantic formulation cannot declare the KKT rank, kernel-positivity,
  symmetry, pairing, multiplier, or solver assumptions;
- an unsupported or incomplete request cannot receive the required
  formulation diagnostic;
- the multiplier conversion and nine serial block-provenance records never
  enter a compilation manifest; and
- an owned compilation session cannot own a returned KKT service.

The v1 compiler document still describes only reduced DTO and canonical
supplied-OTD compilation. This is consistent with the code, but inconsistent
with the roadmap's completed “first registered target” and with the normative
requirement that the compiler preserve these decisions.

### Required outcome

Add one bounded semantic/compiler route for the selected scalar KKT target.
It may be a distinct `CompiledQuadraticKKTProblemT` or another equally explicit
formulation product, but it must not be hidden inside `CompiledProblemT`'s
reduced DTO API. The resolved request and manifest must own:

- formulation and DTO/supplied-OTD construction provenance;
- primal/multiplier/residual layouts and pairings;
- multiplier-to-adjoint conversion;
- rank, kernel-positivity, and symmetry declarations;
- exact action and assembled-block provenance; and
- the selected Krylov and identity-preconditioner policy.

Unsupported targets, missing assumptions, or incompatible pairings must
return stable formulation diagnostics before construction. Do not infer a
generic KKT capability merely because a reduced DTO or supplied-OTD product
exists.

### Required tests

- compile the canonical semantic DTO target to the distinct KKT product;
- compile the canonical supplied-OTD declaration through the same product
  boundary while retaining different provenance;
- assert the complete structured KKT manifest and its projection from the
  resolved decision;
- reject missing rank/kernel, pairing, conversion, symmetry, and solver
  declarations with exact diagnostics; and
- prove that an unrelated scalar or non-canonical formulation does not acquire
  KKT compilation automatically.

## P6.3-R2 – MINRES compatibility has no declared domain–range pairing

**Severity:** P1 – a product can select MINRES without establishing that its
typed domain and range form one symmetric operator space.

### Evidence

`QuadraticKKTLayoutT` stores five `BlockLayout` pointers. A `BlockLayout`
contains space identifiers and dimensions, but no pairing or map that
identifies:

- primal blocks with their stationarity duals; or
- multiplier blocks with their equality-residual duals.

The generic product constructor validates callback presence, right-hand-side
layouts, conversion callbacks, and assumption strings. It does not validate
the above block correspondence. `supports_minres()` returns true solely when
the caller supplies `QuadraticKKTSymmetry::symmetric_indefinite`.

The serial adapter weakens this further to one scalar check: total flattened
domain dimension must equal total flattened range dimension. It will accept,
for example, different primal/stationarity and multiplier/equality partitions
whose totals happen to match. It then erases the typed layouts into one vector
and passes the operator to `SolverMinRes`.

Nor does the product relate its `apply_d_transpose()` callback or full
transpose callback to the declared $D$ action before advertising symmetry. A
caller can provide inconsistent or nonsymmetric actions, select the symmetric
enum, and pass solver validation. The committed negative policy test changes
only the enum; all action callbacks and layouts remain compatible.

This falls short of the normative “symmetric in its declared pairing” rule and
of the required diagnostic when a requested product cannot provide that
pairing.

### Required outcome and tests

Make domain–range identification explicit and typed. Product construction
must validate the primal/stationarity and multiplier/equality block maps,
dimensions, ordering, and pairings before symmetric-indefinite compatibility
can be declared. The solver seam must consume that validated identification
rather than rely on equal total dimensions.

Retain an author declaration for mathematical symmetry where exhaustive
runtime proof is impossible, but attach it to the exact pairing and action
provenance it covers. Add tests that reject:

- equal-total-dimension layouts with mismatched block partitions;
- swapped or missing primal/stationarity pairings;
- swapped or missing multiplier/equality pairings;
- an inconsistent $D^{\mathsf T}$ or KKT-transpose declaration; and
- MINRES selection without validated symmetric pairing evidence.

GMRES may remain available for a declared nonsymmetric product with compatible
domain/range layouts.

## P6.3-R3 – the supplied-OTD bridge manufactures quadratic validity

**Severity:** P1 – the public adapter can turn a nonlinear or nonsymmetric
supplied system into a product advertised as a valid symmetric quadratic KKT
operator.

### Evidence

`make_canonical_supplied_otd_kkt_product()` accepts any
`SuppliedOTDSystemT`. That type requires three variable and three residual
blocks plus value/JVP/VJP/solve callbacks, but it does not declare that the
residual is affine, that its JVP is constant, that the selected block
derivatives have the canonical KKT signs, or that the resulting $Q$ is
symmetric.

The adapter nevertheless:

- evaluates every JVP at the zero point and reuses that action for all KKT
  points;
- evaluates the residual at zero to manufacture both right-hand sides;
- declares full row rank and kernel positivity with constant strings;
- hard-codes $`\lambda_{h}=-p_{h}`$; and
- always selects `QuadraticKKTSymmetry::symmetric_indefinite`.

Those operations are correct for the selected affine scalar system, and its
positive tests support that one target. They are not valid consequences of
the public input contract. A point-dependent JVP is silently frozen at zero;
a nonsymmetric block system is still made MINRES-eligible; and undeclared rank
or kernel conditions become asserted facts instead of a formulation
diagnostic. The function name “canonical” does not enforce those
preconditions.

### Required outcome

Require a typed canonical-quadratic adapter declaration supplied by the
formulation/compiled product. It must identify the block selection, affine or
constant-linearization guarantee, sign and multiplier conversion,
$D$/$D^{\mathsf T}$ pairings, symmetry evidence, and rank/kernel assumptions.
The adapter may then perform the selected JVP/VJP extraction. Without that
declaration it must return a formulation diagnostic or be unavailable.

Do not attempt to infer constancy by sampling a few points. Nonlinear or
non-canonical supplied systems require their own declared formulation product
and nonlinear all-at-once method.

### Required tests

- retain action/residual equivalence for the canonical affine target;
- reject a supplied system with a point-dependent JVP;
- reject nonsymmetric or sign-incompatible selected blocks for the symmetric
  adapter;
- reject absent rank, kernel-positivity, and multiplier declarations; and
- verify that a declared nonsymmetric adapter remains GMRES-only.

## P6.3-R4 – the supplied bridge does not retain owned-session lifetime

**Severity:** P1 – a KKT product adapted from a compiled supplied system can
outlive the mesh owner required by its callback-held deal.II model.

### Evidence

The bridge copies the input `SuppliedOTDSystemT` into a new `shared_ptr` and
captures that copy in every KKT callback. It has no lifetime-owner argument or
token. The P6.2 compiled supplied product owns its deal.II compilation session
separately from the system; the system callbacks capture the scalar model, but
not the session that owns the triangulation observed by the model's
`DoFHandler` objects.

Therefore adapting `compiled.supplied_otd_problem->system()` and then
destroying the compiled wrapper leaves the KKT product with callbacks and a
model but no mesh owner. This remains unsafe even after correcting the P6.2
wrapper's own member-destruction order: the detached bridge copy still does
not receive the owner.

The P6.3 deal.II tests construct a caller-owned triangulation and keep it alive
for the entire model, supplied system, KKT product, and solver scope. They do
not exercise the compiler-owned session path.

### Required outcome and tests

Make an executable supplied system self-own every resource required by its
callbacks, or make the bridge accept an owner-bearing supplied product and
retain its lifetime token in the returned KKT product. The ownership rule
must be part of the reusable boundary rather than a convention of one caller.

Add a sanitizer regression that compiles with an owned session, creates the
KKT bridge, destroys the source compilation result and session handles,
executes KKT/residual/transpose/solve actions, converts the multiplier, and
then destroys the detached KKT product.

## P6.3-R5 – bridge transpose and solver edge reporting lack evidence

**Severity:** P2 – the selected happy path is exercised, but distinct
implementations and report branches can regress without failing the committed
tests.

### Evidence

The backend-neutral product test checks a manually supplied KKT transpose, and
the serial DTO test compares the DTO product transpose with its assembled
matrix. No test calls `apply_kkt_transpose()` on the canonical supplied-OTD
bridge. Its VJP seed mapping and multiplier sign are a separate implementation
introduced in Unit 5, so the other transpose tests are not an oracle for it.

The Unit 6 solver test checks successful DTO/supplied MINRES and GMRES solves,
algorithm names, and solution agreement. It does not independently assert the
reported stationarity/equality norms, a linear failure with residuals still
present, a residual failure after nominal linear termination, or the
iteration-limit report.

There is also one policy-validation edge defect: both `valid()` and
`validate()` require `gmres_maximum_basis >= 3` even when the selected method
is MINRES and the field is unused. A valid MINRES request can therefore be
rejected because of an irrelevant GMRES setting. The committed policy test
checks the undersized basis only with the default MINRES method and records
that rejection as desired behavior.

### Required outcome and tests

- exercise the supplied bridge transpose pairing against its KKT action for
  dense and deal.II targets;
- compare the supplied and DTO transpose actions including multiplier sign;
- assert the exact stationarity and equality residual fields independently;
- cover converged linear/nonconverged residual, nonconverged linear, and
  iteration-limit reports;
- make GMRES basis validation conditional on GMRES selection; and
- verify that unused solver-family settings do not invalidate another method.

## Remediation handoff

Keep remediation separate from this historical review. A practical sequence
is:

1. define the typed KKT pairing/symmetry and canonical-adapter declarations
   (`R2`, `R3`);
2. register the selected DTO and supplied paths in the semantic compiler and
   manifest (`R1`);
3. make the compiled/bridged resource lifetime transitive and test it under
   sanitizers (`R4`); and
4. close transpose, report, and method-specific policy evidence (`R5`).

The next review batch is P6.4. The roadmap marks it conditional, and no P6.4
implementation commit appears between the P6.3 closure at `b2511f1` and the
P6.5 start at `5783291`; its handoff should verify that deferral against the
recorded benchmark trigger rather than invent an implementation range.

## Acceptance checklist

P6.3 cannot be accepted from the reviewed evidence until all findings are
closed and the following checks hold:

- [ ] the canonical DTO and supplied KKT products are returned through a
      distinct semantic/compiler formulation boundary;
- [ ] the resolved decision and manifest retain KKT construction,
      assumptions, pairings, conversion, block provenance, and solver policy;
- [x] primal/stationarity and multiplier/equality pairings are explicit and
      validated before MINRES can be selected;
- [x] a canonical supplied adapter cannot freeze an undeclared nonlinear JVP
      or manufacture symmetry, sign, rank, and kernel assumptions;
- [x] adapted supplied callbacks transitively retain an owned compilation
      session and mesh;
- [x] DTO and supplied actions, residuals, transposes, solutions, and
      multiplier conversions agree on the selected scalar target;
- [x] linear termination, stationarity, and equality residual outcomes are
      independently verified, including failure branches;
- [x] MINRES and GMRES validate only their relevant policy fields and retain
      stable compatibility diagnostics;
- [x] the generic product does not require a scalar PDE family or mass-matrix
      identity;
- [x] the serial assembled realization names all nine block sources and signs;
- [x] P6.4 preconditioner families and flexible GMRES remain excluded; and
- [x] the required neutral, deal.II, and sanitizer verification profiles pass
      after remediation.
